The Maze Wakes

The passage behind you fades, but recovering it takes time. Reach the golden gate before what hunts you closes the distance. The walls will never sit still — but a way through always exists, if you can read it fast enough.

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Press Auto-Pilot to hand control to an AI that will perfectly evade the hunters and solve the maze.

The pattern adapts to your moves. Sitting still will force a shift. The longer you survive, the faster the walls turn and the faster they hunt.
